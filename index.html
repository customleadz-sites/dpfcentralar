Straight answers

Questions Truckers Actually Ask

How long does a DPF cleaning take?

Typical turnaround is 24 hours from pickup to delivery. If your filter needs deeper restoration, we'll tell you before we start — no surprises.

How much does it cost compared to a new filter?

A fraction of replacement. New DPFs commonly run thousands of dollars; cleaning restores the filter you already own. Call for an exact quote — it depends on the filter and how plugged it is.

Is the cleaning under warranty?

Yes — real coverage, not a handshake. Solution-flush cleanings carry a 6-month warranty, ultrasonic restorations a 1-year warranty (restoring as much as 99% of the filter's useful life), and Lifetime Guarantee options are available on top-tier restorations. We'll tell you which level your filter actually needs.

Do you clean DOCs and SCRs too?

Yes — we service the full aftertreatment system: DPF, DOC, SCR and EGR, plus sensors, gaskets, clamps and replacement parts. We also take old filters through the free DPF recycling program.

Do you do DPF deletes?

No. Deletes are illegal for on-road trucks and can cost you at DOT inspection. Cleaning is the legal fix — it restores performance and keeps you compliant.
